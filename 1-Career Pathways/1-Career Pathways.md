## Explore Career Pathways

### I. Objectives
At the end of this activity, you should be able to:
1. Identify the major computing career pathways introduced in lecture and describe what each one involves.
2. Choose one computing career pathway that best matches your interests and explain why.
3. Map a realistic entry-to-expert roadmap for your chosen pathway, including representative job titles and key skills at each stage.
4. Compare your current skills against your chosen pathway's requirements and identify a concrete plan to close the gap.

### II. Materials Needed
* Your CC 101 Unit 2 lecture slides/notes on Computing Career Pathways
* This worksheet (printed copy, or the digital file to fill out and submit)
* Optional: internet access, for Part E (real job posting search)

### III. Background
Computing is not just "programming." As introduced in the Unit 2 lecture, the field branches into several distinct career pathways, each with its own operational scope, required technical competencies, representative job titles, and professional standard or reference body: Software Engineering, Systems & Cloud Infrastructure, Data Science & AI Engineering, Cybersecurity & Threat Defense, and Information Systems & Enterprise IT. Each pathway also has its own career roadmap—the entry-level, mid-level, senior-level, and expert/leadership roles a professional typically grows through. This activity asks you to review those five pathways, choose the one that interests you most, and map out what growing in that pathway could look like for you.

### IV. Procedure

#### Part A - Recall the Five Pathways
1. Using your lecture notes or slides, complete Table 1 by writing the core operational scope and one to two representative job titles for each of the five computing career pathways.

#### Part B - Choose Your Pathway
2. From the five pathways, choose ONE that interests you the most.
3. In two to three sentences, explain why you chose it (e.g., skills you already enjoy, a subject you like, a job title that excites you).

#### Part C - Map Your Career Roadmap
4. If your chosen pathway is Software Engineering, Systems & Cloud Infrastructure, Data Science & AI Engineering, or Cybersecurity & Threat Defense, use the career roadmap shown for it in the lecture. If you chose Information Systems & Enterprise IT (no roadmap was shown in lecture), research typical entry-level, mid-level, senior-level, and expert/leadership roles for this pathway online.
5. Complete Table 2 with example job titles and key skills/responsibilities at each of the four career stages.

#### Part D - Skill Gap Self-Assessment
6. Looking at the essential technical competencies for your chosen pathway (Table 1 / lecture), list at least five in Table 3. For each, mark whether you already have some experience with it, and briefly note how you plan to learn it if you don't.

#### Part E - Real-World Job Search (Optional / Bonus)
7. Search a real job platform (e.g., LinkedIn, Indeed, JobStreet, Kalibrr) for one entry-level job posting that matches your chosen pathway. Record it in Table 4.
